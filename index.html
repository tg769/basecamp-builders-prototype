Gear Rental prototype

Basecamp Builders (Group 5), CS 477. Click through it like a phone app.

  1. Tap the 2-person tent on the home screen.
  2. Pick how many and a pickup time, then tap Hold it, free.
  3. Check the hold time, then tap Cancel hold and confirm.
  4. For the other path, go back and tap Plan my trip.

The hours, prices, counts and phone number are made up, like in our paper prototype tests.
